How to breathe the Name

The 216 digits are twelve readings of one clock — twelve rows of eighteen, and each row is six triplets. Take one row per breath: inhale while the row gathers, and on the exhale sound its six triplets as Hebrew letter-names — Aleph, Bet, Gimel…

The letters ℵ (1), &he; (5), &vav; (6) are the breath-letters — sound them on the air itself. There are exactly seventy-two of them. The breath ripples; it does not march.

Twelve breaths. Five clock-stations pass beneath each breath — sixty in all. After every third breath, a longer silence: the four zeros that were removed from the clock, at the quarter-hours.
